03 — Philosophy

What I Value & My Principles

The personal compass that guides how I make decisions, invest time, and treat the people around me.

Core Values

  • Continuous Growth Always learning, improving, and adapting rather than settling into early comfort.
  • Discipline Over Motivation Staying consistent and showing up every single day even when excitement or motivation fades.
  • Insatiable Curiosity Taking things apart to understand how and why they actually work under the hood.
  • Independence & Self-Reliance Acquiring high-leverage skills to create my own opportunities instead of waiting for permission.
  • Authenticity & Substance Valuing real capability and proof over performative presentation or vanity claims.

Guiding Principles

01 Keep Growing

I want to keep learning rather than becoming comfortable. Comfort is the quiet enemy of skill and ambition.

02 Build, Don't Pretend

Real skills and practical results over simply claiming expertise. Let the code, projects, and execution speak for themselves.

03 Stay Curious

Keep questioning and understanding things beyond the surface. Curiosity is the root of all genuine engineering and creativity.

04 Create Opportunity

Become capable enough to open doors for myself and others. The best way to predict the future is to actively build it.
