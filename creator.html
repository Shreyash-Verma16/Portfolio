🎥 As A Content Creator

01

What I Create: Turning complicated technology into understandable stories

I create technology-focused content that makes complicated technical ideas understandable, interesting, and memorable. My content explores the hidden mechanisms, engineering decisions, limitations, and unexpected behaviors behind the technology we use every day.

I don't just explain what something does. I explore:

> Why does it work this way?

> What is happening underneath?

> What problem led to this design?

> What happens when the system reaches its limits?

02

How I Explain Technology: Simple without making it inaccurate

My approach is to take a technically accurate concept and reduce it to the simplest mental model that still preserves the important idea. I balance: Technical Accuracy × Simplicity × Curiosity.

Problem ➜ Analogy ➜ Tech ➜ Surprise

How My Content Is Generated

Step 01

Brainstorming & Research

Step 04

Publishing to YouTube and Instagram while actively evaluating audience retention metrics and comments to continuously improve production quality.
